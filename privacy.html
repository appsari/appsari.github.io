Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Quran Scroll iOS application ("the App") and services provided by placebonocebo.com ("we," "our," or "us").

1. No Personal Data Collection

We believe your spiritual reflections and reading habits are completely private. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, analytics, or device identifiers.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register or provide any personal details to use the App.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: The App contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking scripts.

2. Local On-Device Storage

All data you create within the App—including your saved bookmarks, personal reflection notes, and reading theme preferences—is stored exclusively on your device. We cannot view or access your notes or bookmarks.

3. Document Exports & Sharing

The App enables you to export your notes (into PDF, Word, Excel, Pages, and Numbers formats) and share verses using the native iOS Share Sheet. Exported files are processed locally on your device and saved only where you choose.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because we do not collect any data from any user, the App complies with all applicable privacy laws, including COPPA and GDPR.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via https://placebonocebo.com.
