Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about reading and journaling with Quran Scroll on iOS.

What makes Quran Scroll different from standard Quran apps?

Standard Quran apps are designed like reference books with dense chapters and menus. Quran Scroll is designed for daily mindfulness and contemplation: it displays a single English verse centered on a full-screen card, allowing you to reflect deeply on one verse at a time without distraction.

How does the random verse discovery work?

Quran Scroll uses a smart non-sequential selection algorithm across all 6,236 verses (114 Surahs). It ensures you don’t see verses from the exact same Surah twice in a row and remembers verses you have already seen during your session so every swipe feels fresh.

Can I export my personal notes to Word, Excel, PDF, Pages, or Numbers?

Yes. You can export either an individual note or your entire collection of notes at any time. Quran Scroll natively generates formatted PDF files, Microsoft Word (.docx), Microsoft Excel (.xlsx), Apple Pages-compatible Rich Text (.rtf), and Apple Numbers-compatible spreadsheets (.csv).

Does the app collect any personal data or require an internet connection?

No. The complete English Quran database is bundled inside the app so it works 100% offline. We do not require user accounts, we do not use analytics or tracking SDKs, and your bookmarks and personal notes never leave your device unless you choose to export or share them.
